ltv-cohort-kit

Repeat-purchase cohort tables and CAC-payback LTV curves from a Shopify or WooCommerce order export. Deterministic math, defined below on the right, verifiable in a spreadsheet. Everything runs in this page — no data leaves your machine (check DevTools > Network: zero requests).

    Methodology — exact definitions

    Cohort
    Calendar month of a customer's first order, using the date written in the export (no timezone conversion). Customers are keyed on the mapped email/ID, lowercased.
    Order dedupe
    Shopify exports one row per line item; rows are grouped by order ID and the first non-empty total is used, so line items never double-count.
    Net revenue
    Order total − refunded amount. Refund-column amounts reduce the original order's month (the export has no refund date, so we never guess one). Standalone negative-total rows reduce the month they occur in.
    LTV
    LTV(k) = cumulative net revenue through month-offset k ÷ cohort size × gross margin %.
    CAC
    Monthly marketing spend ÷ new customers acquired that month. No spend entered → “CAC unavailable”, never 0. Spend in a month with zero new customers is flagged, not divided.
    Payback month
    First observed offset k where cumulative margin-adjusted LTV per customer ≥ CAC.
    Right-censoring
    Months not fully elapsed at the as-of date — including the current partial month — are hatched/masked, never shown as zeros or decay.
    Retention
    Share of the cohort with ≥1 order of total ≥ $0 in the month. $0 orders count for retention but add no revenue; refunds/negative rows never count as activity.
    Exclusions
    Rows with a blank customer identifier can't be cohorted: they are excluded and the count and revenue share are reported. Above 5% of revenue, results are flagged unreliable.
    Currencies
    Mixed currency codes/symbols trigger a warning; amounts are never silently summed across currencies. Dot-decimal amounts only; “99,00” style is rejected per row.
    Privacy
    This page makes zero network requests — no analytics, fonts, or CDN. Auditable in DevTools → Network.
